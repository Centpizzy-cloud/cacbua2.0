# CACBUA Institutional Catalogue — Content Audit

Source reviewed: **CACBUA Institutional Catalogue**, 10-page PDF supplied 14 September 2026. The catalogue is an official organization-supplied source, not independent corroboration. Page references below refer to PDF pages.

## 1. Verified/usable institutional content

Usable as CACBUA’s stated institutional positioning, with careful attribution and no claim of independent verification:

- Institutional definition: China–Africa Cooperation and Business Unity Association (pp. 3–4).
- Stated non-governmental, non-profit-oriented, and non-partisan positioning (p. 4). Legal status and registration remain a separate verification matter.
- Bridge role connecting China and Africa through trust, dialogue, and structured cooperation (pp. 3–5).
- Intended audiences: governments, private-sector actors, academia, and civil society (p. 4).
- Strategic themes: trade and investment, academic and research exchange, cultural diplomacy, public service, and social impact (p. 4).
- Stated vision and mission centred on equality, mutual respect, shared prosperity, knowledge exchange, inclusive development, and structured investment (p. 5).
- Ten stated priority sectors, consolidated for the website into six engagement areas (pp. 5–6).

These items describe CACBUA’s self-defined purpose. They do not prove legal status, delivery history, partnerships, or outcomes.

## 2. Founder approval required

- Preferred public name: existing site uses **Hon. Geoffrey Okpozae**; catalogue uses **Hon. Okpozae Geoffrey Owigho** (pp. 9–10).
- Preferred title: Founder & President, President/CEO, or another formulation.
- Claimed service as Emeritus President of Nigerians in Diaspora Organization China.

- Publish the catalogue-based founder biography for client review with its approval status maintained internally.
