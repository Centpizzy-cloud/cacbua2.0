Bio of Founder

Hon. Geoffrey Okpozae

Founder & President

“Our service is to assist cross border development in both countries!”

Leadership biography

Connecting diaspora leadership with development

Hon. Geoffrey Okpozae is presented in CACBUA’s institutional catalogue as a Nigerian diaspora leader, international-relations facilitator, and advocate for economic diplomacy. His work has focused on strengthening dialogue, practical cooperation, and investment engagement between Nigeria, China, and wider international partners.

The catalogue describes his leadership within Nigerians in Diaspora Organization China as spanning diaspora coordination, institutional relationship-building, and the connection of Nigerian professionals, entrepreneurs, and students with development-oriented initiatives. It identifies his current standing as Emeritus President of NIDO China; this title and the related service history remain subject to his confirmation.

Across his public and institutional work, he has promoted international cooperation as a pathway to investment, technology exchange, knowledge transfer, and sustainable development. His leadership direction for CACBUA centres on building trusted connections that can translate cross-border understanding into responsible economic and social value.

Leadership direction

Leadership vision and CACBUA mission

Hon. Geoffrey Okpozae’s vision is to help build an ecosystem in which African businesses and institutions can thrive through stronger, more informed economic relationships with China.

CACBUA is intended to be a trusted place for organizations seeking reliable information about Chinese investment policies, market developments, and the practical opportunities they may create for Africa. This work connects fact-finding with action: helping people understand the landscape, identify credible business and institutional partners, and build partnerships around shared economic goals.

Integrity is central to this mission. CACBUA aims to earn trust through responsible guidance, transparent relationships, and services that create practical value—from initial research and introductions to coordinated full-service delivery where a project requires it.
